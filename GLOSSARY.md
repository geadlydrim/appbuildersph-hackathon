# CommuteNity

An on-device commute assistant for Metro Manila riders. It answers trip questions offline from data stored on the phone, and gets better from community contributions when the phone is online.

## Language

### Commute data

**Commute pack**:
The versioned bundle of places, stops, routes, segments, and transfers stored on the phone. It is the only source of facts in an answer.
_Avoid_: dataset, database, catalog

**Place**:
A name a rider might say, such as a landmark, area, station, or terminal. It resolves to one or more stops.
_Avoid_: location, POI

**Stop**:
A point where a rider boards or alights.
_Avoid_: station (unless it's a rail station), node

**Route**:
One transit line that a single vehicle type runs, e.g. a specific jeepney line or the MRT-3. It never means a door-to-door plan.
_Avoid_: line (in prose), path

**Segment**:
One stop-to-stop piece of a route, carrying its fare and minutes.
_Avoid_: edge, hop

**Transfer**:
A walk between two nearby stops that links routes.
_Avoid_: connection

**Corridor**:
A stretch of the city whose routes and stops the pack covers in depth. The demo runs on corridors.
_Avoid_: area, zone

**Source class**:
Where a fact came from. `collected` means verified during the event, `known` means team knowledge, and `mock` means synthetic. Mock facts are always marked as sample data.
_Avoid_: real/fake, verified flag

### Answers

**Trip**:
A door-to-door plan from an origin place to a destination place, made of legs.
_Avoid_: route, journey, itinerary, trip option

**Leg**:
One part of a trip spent on a single route (or walking), from a board stop to an alight stop.
_Avoid_: step, segment

**Candidate**:
A trip considered for an answer. It is either generated by the algorithm from the pack or taken from a valid suggestion.
_Avoid_: option, result

**Best trip**:
The candidate the app auto-picks as most efficient and shows first.
_Avoid_: best route, recommended route

**Alternative**:
Any other candidate shown when the rider asks for more, labelled Algorithm or Community.
_Avoid_: other routes

**Para point**:
The stop or landmark where a rider gets off a leg.
_Avoid_: drop-off, destination (for a leg)

### Community

**Suggestion**:
A trip a rider submits for an origin–destination pair. It is built only from pack routes and stops.
_Avoid_: post, submission, contributed route

**Vote**:
A rider's "worked" or "didn't work" mark on a trip.
_Avoid_: like, rating, upvote

**Contribution**:
A suggestion or a vote.
_Avoid_: post, user content

### Ranking and evaluation

**Scenario**:
One labelled comparison set for a resolved origin–destination pair and stated preference, containing exactly three candidates.
_Avoid_: test case, prompt

**Preference label**:
One rater's strict order of a scenario's three candidates. It yields pairwise examples for ranker training.
_Avoid_: vote, score
